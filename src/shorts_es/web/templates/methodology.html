Methodology

What this dataset is

An executable verification layer over the public net-short-position disclosures published by the CNMV in NetShortPositions.xls. shorts-es downloads the workbook, stores it immutably by SHA-256, parses it deterministically and exposes the contents — with a complete provenance chain from every number back to a snapshot, sheet and row.

What it is not

  • Not total short interest. Public disclosures cover only positions notified to CNMV; undisclosed short exposure is invisible to this dataset (and to the source).
  • Not a trading signal. No inference, scoring or prediction is applied.
  • Not an official CNMV product. The source always prevails; discrepancies must be resolved against the original publication.

Threshold semantics

Under Regulation (EU) 236/2012, net short positions of 0.5 % or more in listed shares must be publicly disclosed. CNMV, however, publishes all notified values — including positions below 0.5 % and explicit 0.00 % closing notifications. shorts-es preserves the published figure verbatim (arbitrary decimal precision, no rounding) and never treats a sub-0.5 % publication as absent.

Temporal semantics

Two clocks are kept separate:

  • Position date (effective/regulatory time): the date the source attributes to the position.
  • Observed at (knowledge time): when shorts-es first downloaded a snapshot containing the disclosure.

Historical rows present in our first snapshot are marked RECONSTRUCTED_HISTORICAL: we know they existed in that publication, but we cannot claim when they were first published or that they were publicly visible on their position date.

Reconstruction and verification

For each (LEI, ISIN, holder) pair, the latest publication by position date defines its published state. The Series sheet is used to reconstruct that state independently and compare it with the Current sheet published by CNMV — shorts-es verify reports matches, missing, unexpected and conflicting pairs. A pair absent from the Current sheet is not currently published; it is never reported as zero.

Coverage

Whatever CNMV publishes in the workbook — Spanish and non-Spanish ISINs under CNMV competence. Absence of an issuer from the file does not imply CNMV is (or is not) its relevant competent authority.

Known limitations

  • The workbook carries no per-row publication timestamp; knowledge before our first snapshot is unknowable to us.
  • The Previous sheet contains duplicated physical rows; they collapse into one canonical disclosure and retain multiple provenance rows.
  • The source contains ISIN case-typo variants (eS…); they are normalized to upper case for identity.
  • Pairs can leave the published list without a closing notification.
